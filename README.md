# Healthcare Operations Analytics

End-to-end healthcare operations analytics project combining business analysis, SQL, data visualization, KPI analysis, and data-driven recommendations.

## Project Overview

This independent portfolio project analyzes healthcare appointment operations to identify patterns in appointment outcomes, scheduling, patient access, wait times, patient satisfaction, and provider workload.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow from business problem definition and requirements gathering through SQL analysis, Power BI visualization, and business recommendations.

## Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Understanding overall appointment performance
- Identifying cancellation and no-show patterns
- Evaluating scheduling lead time
- Analyzing wait times and patient satisfaction
- Comparing performance across specialties and locations
- Understanding provider workload
- Identifying opportunities for operational improvement

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall appointment completion, cancellation, and no-show performance?
2. Which specialties have higher no-show rates?
3. Which locations have higher cancellation rates?
4. Does scheduling lead time relate to missed appointments?
5. How do wait times vary across operations?
6. How does patient satisfaction vary with wait time?
7. How is scheduled workload distributed across providers?
8. Which appointment types show different performance patterns?
