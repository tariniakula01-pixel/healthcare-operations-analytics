# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis identifies several operational patterns across appointment scheduling, access, wait times, patient experience, and provider workload.

The findings are based on the synthetic appointment dataset used for this independent portfolio project.

## Key Findings

### 1. Missed appointments increase with longer scheduling lead times

Appointments scheduled 31+ days in advance show the highest combined missed-appointment activity, with both no-show and cancellation rates higher than shorter lead-time groups.

**Recommendation:** Review scheduling practices for appointments booked far in advance and evaluate whether reminder or confirmation processes could reduce missed appointments.

### 2. No-show rates vary across specialties

Dermatology has the highest observed no-show rate among the specialties analyzed, followed by Pediatrics and Primary Care.

**Recommendation:** Investigate specialty-specific scheduling and reminder patterns and consider targeted interventions for areas with higher no-show rates.

### 3. Cancellation rates vary by location

Columbia has the highest observed cancellation rate, while Baltimore has a lower cancellation rate but a higher no-show rate than Columbia.

**Recommendation:** Review location-level appointment access, scheduling practices, and cancellation patterns to identify operational differences.

### 4. Longer wait times are associated with lower patient satisfaction

Patient satisfaction decreases consistently across the wait-time categories, from the highest satisfaction among patients waiting 15 minutes or less to the lowest satisfaction among patients waiting more than 60 minutes.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize opportunities to reduce extended wait times and investigate operational bottlenecks contributing to longer patient waits.

### 5. Provider workload varies

Scheduled appointment minutes differ across providers, indicating differences in scheduled workload.

**Recommendation:** Review workload distribution alongside appointment volume and duration to identify opportunities for better capacity balancing.

## Priority Recommendations

1. Review appointments scheduled 31+ days in advance.
2. Investigate high no-show specialties, particularly Dermatology and Pediatrics.
3. Analyze location-level cancellation and no-show patterns.
4. Identify operational causes of extended patient wait times.
5. Review provider workload distribution for potential capacity-balancing opportunities.

## Important Note

These findings describe observed patterns within a synthetic dataset. Relationships identified in the analysis should be treated as associations rather than evidence of causation.
