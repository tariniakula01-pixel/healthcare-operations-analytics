# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

The stakeholder analysis identifies the key groups involved in healthcare appointment operations and defines their interests, concerns, influence, and information needs.

## Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Role / Responsibility | Key Interest | Information Needed | Influence | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare Operations Leadership | Oversees overall operational performance | Operational efficiency and patient access | Executive KPIs, trends, problem areas, recommendations | High | High |
| Clinic Managers | Manage day-to-day clinic operations | Wait times, appointment capacity, cancellations, no-shows | Location and specialty performance | High | High |
| Provider Management | Monitors provider capacity and performance | Provider utilization and appointment demand | Provider-level KPIs and workload trends | High | High |
| Patient Access Team | Manages appointment scheduling and access | Appointment availability and missed appointments | Scheduling patterns, lead time, cancellation and no-show trends | Medium | High |
| Business Operations Team | Supports process improvement initiatives | Process efficiency and performance improvement | Root causes, process metrics, improvement opportunities | Medium | Medium |
| Data & Reporting Team | Maintains reporting and analytical processes | Data quality and reporting consistency | Data definitions, KPI calculations, source data | Medium | Medium |

## Stakeholder Prioritization

### High Influence / High Interest

**Healthcare Operations Leadership**

Requires a concise view of overall performance and actionable recommendations to support operational decisions.

**Clinic Managers**

Need detailed operational information to identify problems within their locations and specialties.

**Provider Management**

Needs provider-level performance information to understand capacity and utilization.

### Medium Influence / High Interest

**Patient Access Team**

Requires appointment and scheduling insights to identify opportunities to improve patient access and reduce missed appointments.

### Medium Influence / Medium Interest

**Business Operations Team**

Uses analytical findings to support process improvement initiatives.

**Data & Reporting Team**

Supports data preparation, KPI consistency, and reporting requirements.

## Stakeholder Needs Summary

The analysis should provide different levels of information depending on stakeholder needs:

- **Leadership:** High-level KPIs, trends, and recommendations
- **Clinic Managers:** Location and specialty performance
- **Provider Management:** Provider utilization and appointment patterns
- **Patient Access:** Scheduling lead time, cancellations, and no-shows
- **Business Operations:** Process inefficiencies and improvement opportunities
- **Data & Reporting:** Consistent definitions, calculations, and data quality

## Key Stakeholder Considerations

The final solution should:

1. Present clear and consistently defined KPIs.
2. Allow performance to be compared across relevant operational dimensions.
3. Highlight areas requiring further investigation.
4. Separate operational metrics from recommendations.
5. Provide sufficient detail for operational teams while remaining easy for leadership to interpret.
