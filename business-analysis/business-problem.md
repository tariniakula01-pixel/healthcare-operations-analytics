# Healthcare Operations & Patient Access Analytics

## Project Type

Independent Portfolio Project

## Business Problem

A healthcare organization is experiencing operational challenges related to appointment management and patient access. Leadership wants a clearer understanding of appointment demand, cancellations, no-shows, patient wait times, provider utilization, and patient satisfaction.

Currently, operational performance is difficult to evaluate consistently across specialties, locations, providers, and appointment types. Without a consolidated view of these metrics, management may have difficulty identifying the primary causes of missed appointments, long patient wait times, and differences in operational performance.

The organization needs a data-driven approach to identify operational trends, measure key performance indicators, uncover areas of inefficiency, and prioritize opportunities for improvement.

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate healthcare appointment operations and provide actionable insights that can help improve:

- Patient access
- Appointment completion
- No-show and cancellation rates
- Patient wait times
- Provider utilization
- Patient satisfaction
- Overall operational efficiency

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall appointment completion rate?
2. What are the cancellation and no-show rates?
3. Which specialties have the highest rates of missed appointments?
4. Which locations have the longest average patient wait times?
5. Does scheduling lead time appear to influence cancellations or no-shows?
6. Which appointment types have the strongest and weakest operational performance?
7. Which providers or locations show unusually high wait times?
8. How does patient satisfaction vary with patient wait time?
9. Which operational areas should leadership prioritize for improvement?

## Stakeholders

The analysis is designed to support the needs of:

- Healthcare Operations Leadership
- Clinic Managers
- Provider Management
- Patient Access Teams
- Business Operations Teams
- Data & Reporting Teams

## Expected Deliverables

The project will produce:

- Business requirements
- Stakeholder analysis
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- As-Is and To-Be process analysis
- SQL-based data analysis
- KPI definitions
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Business insights
- Data-driven recommendations

## Success Measures

The project will be considered successful if stakeholders can use the resulting analysis to:

- Identify major appointment-management issues
- Compare operational performance across specialties and locations
- Understand patterns associated with missed appointments
- Identify opportunities to reduce patient wait times
- Monitor key operational KPIs
- Make evidence-based improvement decisions
